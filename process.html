3. Inside a lab — the two clocks

The Tuesday lab (teach, then build on the training case):

flowchart LR
    O["0-10<br/>Open: tools alive,<br/>defend, gap check"] --> T["10-45<br/>Teach: the concept,<br/>the worked example in digits"]
    T --> D["45-80 · D · the question,<br/>the same for everyone"] --> R["R · what you work from"] --> I["I · build from the request<br/>or the contract"]
    I --> F["While it runs:<br/>gap list in a second chat"] --> V["V · the known answer:<br/>match, or debug with your AI"]
    V --> RF["80-87<br/>Learn on your own ·<br/>Reflect to a partner"] --> B["87-90<br/>Before Thursday:<br/>the list on the week page"]

The Thursday lab (make it yours, prove it on an input you did not choose):

flowchart LR
    O["0-10<br/>Open"] --> M["10-70<br/>Make it yours: the design that is the student's,<br/>then the partner's pick: run unchanged<br/>on a release nobody on the page named"]
    M --> P["the pick's report"]
    P --> A["Partner checks the report<br/>against the filings"] --> RF["70-80<br/>Reflect"] --> CK["80-90<br/>Checkout: submit individually<br/>or one upload per team"]

Exceptions the instructor calls in the room: Session 3's partner pick is called at minute 40. Sessions 10 to 12 run the building clock instead: a ten-minute stand-up, seventy minutes of building with the instructor walking the room, a ten-minute close; Session 10's exchange run is inside the build.

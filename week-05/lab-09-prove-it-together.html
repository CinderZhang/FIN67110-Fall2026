Lab 09 — Prove It Together

One thing today: prove the joined desk on names nobody on your team chose, measure what the join changed, and file the team proposal.

You arrive with: one command that runs your desk end to end; the Checkpoint 3 draft; this week's live release chosen, and who runs it.

Open — the paired review (10 minutes)

  1. Tools alive. The one command runs; the replay runs with the network off.
  2. Defend. Read your partner the paragraph of your draft proposal that says which join your desk uses where. Partner points at the arrow in your diagram that it describes.
  3. Gap check. Write one line: what in your final report comes from the worksheet, and what would be there without it?

I — make it yours, then prove it general

  1. Own the join. As a team, write which join your desk uses where, in one line at the top of the repository's README, under the thing you own. If the tool join is yours, show the sentence in the role's instructions that lets it quote and forbids it to recompute.
  2. The partner's names. Run the desk, one command each, on the two names your partner picked. Expect: two run folders; two final reports with a view; the filings on each first line; the spinoff parts "not applicable" where the name is not one.
  3. The three-column table, on one of the two: the parts alone (the team's verified report and the worksheet's output, run as Week 4 ran them), the joined desk's final report, the filing. Rows: the eleven core claims, the view, the one number. Mark every row where the first two columns differ, and check each marked row against the third. The shape, not your answer: diluted EPS, GAAP | [value] | [value] | [the filing's line]: same; the view | not computed | more than the price | the worksheet's low case, [filing], [section].
  4. The proposal. As a team, finish the Checkpoint 3 page from the shape below.
  5. The live report. If this week's release has filed, run the desk on it now, into runs/live, and upload the verified report with the view under it to Brightspace → Live record before its window closes. If it files later this week, the member you chose runs it then.
  6. While it runs: one item from your gap list, in a second AI chat. Never collected.

Evaluation — Learn on your own

  1. What is it (the same question run two ways, compared on a fixed list of claims)?
  2. How does it work here (the three-column table is one; the hit-rate table is another; what each can and cannot show)?
  3. Explain it: what would a skill have to change in the table for your team to keep it, and what would make you remove it?

Reflect

Explain two things to your partner. First, show the row of the three-column table that surprised you. Second, name the role you took, and the part of the desk you understand least.
